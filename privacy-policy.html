PubHub — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PubHub ("the App"), developed by Duy Anh Nguyen, handles information when you use it to publish video content to YouTube, Facebook, and TikTok.

1. Information We Collect

PubHub is a single-user, locally-run application. It processes: video files you choose to upload, selected and stored locally on your own device; OAuth access tokens issued by YouTube, Facebook, and TikTok after you log in and authorize the App, stored locally and used only to publish content on your behalf; and, optionally, transcripts generated locally from your videos and basic product/content metadata (e.g. product name, category) extracted from those transcripts, stored in a local database on your device.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to operate the App's core functionality: creating and publishing video posts to the platforms you connect, generating platform-specific titles, descriptions, and hashtags, and, if you opt in, transcribing your videos and extracting content metadata for your own searchable local reference.

3. Data Storage and Sharing

All data processed by PubHub — video files, transcripts, and the product database — is stored locally on your own device. We do not operate a remote server that stores your videos or personal data, and we do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, other than the platforms (YouTube, Facebook, TikTok) you explicitly direct the App to publish to using your own authorization.

4. Third-Party APIs

PubHub connects to the YouTube Data API, Meta Graph API, and TikTok Content Posting API using OAuth authorization that you grant directly. Use of these platforms is also subject to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you control retention directly — deleting the App or its local data folder removes all stored videos, transcripts, and tokens. You may also revoke the App's access at any time directly from your YouTube, Facebook, or TikTok account security settings.

6. Security

Access tokens and credentials are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to any server other than the official platform APIs (YouTube, Facebook, TikTok) for the sole purpose of publishing content you authorize.

7. Children's Privacy

PubHub is not directed at children and is not intended for use by individuals under the age required by each connected platform's own terms of service.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
